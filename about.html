About

Who we are, what drives us, and the team behind Aricord.

Deep expertise. A clear focus.

Aricord is built around a simple idea: the best results come from people who truly understand the problem. Our team brings together deep experience in finance, accounting, regulation and SAP, helping organisations navigate complex transformation with greater clarity and confidence.

From S/4HANA and Central Finance to regulatory reporting, BTP and AI, we bring the right expertise at every stage from shaping the solution through to implementation and go-live. We keep things practical, stay close to our clients, and focus on solutions that work in the real world, not just on paper. 150+ years of combined experience. Specialist knowledge. Results that matter.

Mission

To make complex finance transformation simpler by bringing the right expertise, technology and people together.

Vision

Make the complex feel remarkably simple.

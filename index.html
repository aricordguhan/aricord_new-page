What actually slows down a Central Finance go-live

Most CFIN delays trace back to source-system mapping decisions made too late, not the technical build itself. Getting finance and functional teams aligned on mapping rules before realization starts is usually the highest-leverage fix.

Blog · Sample

Reconciliation checkpoints worth building in early

Waiting until UAT to reconcile migrated balances is the most common cause of last-minute cutover panic. Three checkpoints — after mock load, after first parallel run, and pre-cutover — catch most issues while there's still time to fix them.

Blog · Sample

Subledger vs. classic GL: how to decide

FPSL and RAR make sense once sub-ledger-level detail genuinely drives reporting or compliance needs. If that detail isn't consumed anywhere downstream, the added complexity usually isn't worth it yet.

Blog · Sample

BTP for finance teams: where to start

Most finance-led BTP use cases start small — a single workflow or integration, not a platform rollout. Picking one painful manual handoff to automate first builds the case for anything bigger.

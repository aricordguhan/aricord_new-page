S/4 · CFIN

S/4HANA Finance & Central Finance

Finance transformation, Central Finance, implementation and optimisation — supported by proven accelerators and hands-on delivery experience. We help finance teams simplify their landscape, harmonise reporting and build a single source of truth across group and entity level.

What we deliver

  • Finance transformation roadmaps and S/4HANA readiness assessments
  • Central Finance design, replication and harmonisation
  • Universal Journal, New Asset Accounting and Group Reporting

  • Post-implementation reviews and finance process optimisation
FPSL · RAR · RE-FX

SAP Subledger & Regulatory Solutions

Specialist expertise across FPSL, FSDP, RAR and RE-FX, with deep experience in complex insurance and banking environments. We turn demanding accounting standards into robust, auditable SAP solutions.

What we deliver

  • IFRS 17 and IFRS 9 subledger design and implementation with FPSL
  • Financial Services Data Platform (FSDP) integration and data modelling
  • IFRS 15 revenue recognition with SAP RAR
  • IFRS 16 lease accounting and real estate management with RE-FX
  • Regulatory reporting, reconciliation and audit-trail design

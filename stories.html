S/4HANA · COManufacturing

SAP S/4HANA transformation success: enhancing manufacturing efficiency at scale

A large manufacturer was facing stability issues after its SAP S/4HANA migration. We helped strengthen operations across Make-To-Order, Make-To-Stock, Engineer-To-Order and margin reporting.

  • 30%reduction in process cycle time
  • 40%improvement in margin accuracy

Challenge

The client struggled to integrate diverse manufacturing processes with SAP S/4HANA. Inefficiencies in MTO, MTS and ETO, and inaccurate margin reporting, caused operational delays and financial inaccuracies.

Outcomes

  • 30% reduction in process cycle time
